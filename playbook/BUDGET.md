## Paid, recommended (about ₹500 now, up to ₹1,500 in reserve)

| Item | Cost | When | Why it pays back |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sarvam credit top-up** | **₹500** (~1,000 minutes at ₹0.49/min) | Only after prompt 14 shows Sarvam beats on-device on our recorded clips | Better code-mixed Hindi and Punjabi accuracy is the core of "Quality of AI" and the live demo |
| **Claude Pro usage credits** (reserve) | **Up to ₹1,000–1,500**, opt-in, pay-as-you-go at API rates | Only if the weekly limit blocks work in the last week (11–14 Dec) | Cheapest way to protect the deadline; no plan upgrade |

## Don't buy
- Claude Max ($100/month): over budget. Teammates' free trial weeks plus Codex and Antigravity cover the gap.
- Motion+, shadcn.io Pro, 21st.dev paid tiers: the free tiers are enough for our screens.
- ElevenLabs: Sarvam is stronger for Indian languages. Revisit only if Punjabi fails in prompt 14.
- Paid hosting: Azure student credit covers it.
- A domain beyond the free `.tech`.
- Any paid Figma seat: Claude Design + Stitch + the free Figma plan cover our hand-off. Note: the Figma MCP's free and Starter limits are low (about 20 tool calls a month on Starter or View seats), so use Figma MCP for reading and hand-off, not heavy canvas writing.
